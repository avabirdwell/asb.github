Overview

During my AmeriCorps service term with the Maine Conservation Corps, I worked as an Environmental Steward and Educator at the Hurricane Island Center for Science and Leadership. My role centered on delivering hands‑on coastal science programming, supporting sustainable aquaculture education, and facilitating stewardship projects for students and community groups across mid‑coast Maine.

My Role

  • Developed and taught coastal ecology and aquaculture lessons for middle‑school and high‑school students.
  • Led field‑based learning experiences on Hurricane Island’s working waterfront and research sites.
  • Facilitated stewardship projects focused on marine debris, shoreline monitoring, and sustainable resource use.
  • Engaged diverse community groups, including visiting land‑trust members and adult learners.
  • Collaborated with educators, researchers, and conservation partners to support environmental literacy.

Impact

My service strengthened my commitment to community‑centered environmental education and deepened my understanding of how coastal communities engage with science, stewardship, and marine resource management. These experiences directly inform my ongoing work in fisheries, shark depredation research, and socio‑ecological systems.
